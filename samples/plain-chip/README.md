# Plain chip concept

Exploration of feedback that the chip has more detail than the rest of the logo. The chip is a solid gold shape with its existing size, position, rotation, and rounded corners. Its internal circuit lines have been removed. The wallet, card, colours, and wordmark are unchanged.

`comparison.png` shows the current design on the left and this concept on the right, on light and dark backgrounds and at smaller sizes.

Each background folder contains a transparent SVG logo and icon, PNG copies, and a `fossbilling-logo-text.svg` with editable lettering (requires the Manrope font in `source/fonts/`). The outlined `fossbilling-logo.svg` renders without an installed font.

These are discussion samples; the production assets in `source/` and `assets/` are unchanged.
