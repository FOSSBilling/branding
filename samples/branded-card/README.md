# Branded card concept

Separate exploration of adding FOSSBilling to the blue card. Starts from the original logo, retaining the detailed gold chip. A white copy of the outlined Manrope wordmark follows the card’s tilt to suggest an issuer name on a payment card. All other artwork is unchanged.

`comparison.png` compares the current and proposed versions on light and dark backgrounds, including smaller sizes. Card lettering becomes too small to read at favicon and header sizes.

Each background folder includes transparent SVG and PNG logo and icon samples, plus a logo with editable main lettering (requires the bundled Manrope font). Card lettering is outlined in every SVG for portable rendering.

Production assets and the separate plain-chip samples are unchanged.
